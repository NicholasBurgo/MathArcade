- (c) Would you be surprised if a randomly selected sample of this type of soil has a bulk density in excess of $2.0\ \text{g/cm}^3$? Explain, based on the probability of this occurring.
- (d) What point has the property that only 10% of the soil samples have bulk density this high or higher?
- (e) What is the moment generating function for $X$?
