# Gaps: test skills the homework doesn't fully cover

The study guide lists skills the test can ask about. The items below are skills where the assigned homework gives no practice, asks only for the result instead of the derivation, or has just one problem.

**Extra practice** lists unassigned problems from the same textbook pages as your homework photos. Their text isn't copied here. The methods are in [what-to-learn.md](../test-section-extraction/what-to-learn.md) section 4.

## No homework practice

### Derive the pdf for a uniform distribution (§4.1)

No assigned problem derives it. 4.1 #10 starts from the uniform pdf and finds the cdf.

- **Extra practice:** 4.2 #18 shows the uniform mean is $(a+b)/2$ and the variance is $(b-a)^2/12$. 4.1 #11 works with a uniform distribution on $[0, 2\pi]$.

### Derive the negative binomial pdf (§3.6)

3.6 #47 and #48 use the pdf but never derive it. The homework also doesn't cover the negative binomial mean, variance or MGF.

- **Extra practice:** 3.6 #49 and #50 use the MGF to find the negative binomial mean and variance. No problem in your photos derives the pdf itself.
